Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Bentonville HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Bentonville HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.
