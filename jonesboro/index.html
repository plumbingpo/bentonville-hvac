Keeping Your Jonesboro HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Jonesboro

HVAC systems in this area face a familiar set of problems.
